THE LINE

The Sukhumvit Line runs 47 stations from Khu Khot (N24) in the north to Kheha (E23) in Samut Prakan. The simulation compresses the route into a continuous elevated viaduct and scales distances for playability. N6 Sena Ruam, an unopened infill station, is not included.

ANorthern suburbs · shop houses, Chatuchak green

DSouthern delta · industry, coast, Erawan
