audio, texture or 3D-model assets. The following runtime libraries and typefaces
are loaded from public CDNs.

## Generated content

All buildings, roads, viaducts, stations, trains, trees, passengers, signs,
billboards, textures, minimap graphics and audio are generated procedurally in
the browser by `script.js`. No external asset bundle is used.
