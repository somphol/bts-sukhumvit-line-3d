# Attribution

## Origin

**BTS Sukhumvit Line 3D** was built as an original project on 2026-11-01/02 in a single OpenCode session (OpenCode 2.2.5, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash). It is not a fork, reskin or copy of an existing repository. The application code, procedural city, train mesh, route, HUD, audio synthesis and self-test were written for this project.

The brief asked for the telemetry and station-progress ideas of `tramtracker-2088` to be adapted to Bangkok's elevated BTS. That project was read as design inspiration for transit telemetry, minimap and arrival-loop concepts. No source code, assets or text were copied from it, and the resulting mechanics are elevated-rail, not tram.

## What is new here

- A 47-station Sukhumvit Line data table, four zone profiles, bilingual station/landmark copy and interchange metadata.
- A compressed 39-point elevated alignment with zone-weighted station chainage, 47 platform pairs, a four-car train and terminus reversal.
- Four camera rigs (cab, passenger window, orbit, platform), a bilingual transit HUD, 47-row station list and canvas minimap.
- A procedural Bangkok with instanced buildings, landmarks, billboards, parks, traffic, rain, lightning, passenger exchange and day/dusk/night presets.
- A Web Audio graph for traction, joints, brakes, chimes, horn, ambience, music and thunder.
- A PA-first door state machine and 145 pre-rendered Thai PA clips.
- A 36-check in-browser self-test and a dependency-free static checker.

## Generated audio

The 145 Thai PA clips under `audio/th/` were generated locally with the macOS **Kanya** Thai voice (`say -v Kanya -r 165`) and encoded to AAC (`afconvert`, 32 kbps). They are original generated recordings, not third-party recordings and not the voice of the real BTS announcer. English PA uses the browser/system speech voice.
